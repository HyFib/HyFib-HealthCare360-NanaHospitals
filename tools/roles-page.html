HealthCare360 roles

What each of the __ROLES__ built-in roles may do on a new installation: its job, its permissions, how much of the server it opens and what it may approve. Written from the code at commit __COMMIT__, __DATE__.

  • __ROLES__built-in roles
  • __PERMISSIONS__permissions
  • __ENDPOINTS__API endpoints that ask for one
  • __APPROVALS__kinds of change a second person approves

These are the defaults. What a hospital changes afterwards (roles of its own, permissions given or taken back, roles turned off) is on its Administration › Roles and permissions screen and in its audit trail, not on this page.

    Switching access

    Who signs in

    Staff

    Doctors, nurses, front office, billing, pharmacy, laboratory, administration and every other department. Also accounts the hospital makes for other care providers that read records over FHIR, with a role of their own that holds fhir.read.

    User name and password, with two-step verification where a role requires it. Administration only from an enrolled HC360 Desktop. Any staff role, built-in or the hospital's own.

    Patients

    Patients and their families, on the patient portal.

    Mobile number and a one-time code. The Patient role only, given at the first sign-in.

    Devices

    Workstations, kiosks, queue displays and lab agents sign in as enrolled devices, not as users. No code creates a service account.

    A new installation has one account, admin, the first Super Administrator, whose one-time password must be changed at the first sign-in.

    Rules every switch follows

    • Narrowing access applies at once. Widening it waits for a second person. Nobody approves their own request, or access given to themselves.
    • The Super Administrator and Patient roles are never turned off. Nobody turns off their own account, or a role they hold.
    • A role that is off still binds its holders: the two-step verification, enrolled workstation and idle lock it requires still apply.
    • Before a role is turned off, the screen says how many people hold it and for how many it is the only role in use.
    • Every change is in the audit trail, with the approval that gave the access.

    Departments have no switch yet

    Three parts of HC360 have a switch: the patient portal, video consultations and ABDM health records. A switch per department (pharmacy, laboratory, billing and the rest) is planned, and is safe only when it also covers the department's background services and the work other departments send it. A switch that only hid screens would leave orders and prescriptions arriving where nobody looks. Until then, a department that is not live is kept closed through its roles.

    Kept true by
    The same test, which writes the tables in docs/security/ROLES.md from the same reading of the code. It runs on every change, on Linux and Windows, and fails when either file no longer matches the code or when one kind of approval has two different approvers.
    API endpoints
    A role opens an endpoint when its default permissions meet every permission rule on it. __ENDPOINTS__ endpoints ask for a permission; the rest are for anyone signed in, or public. The list of routes is left out of this page on purpose.
    Two-step verification
    Required of the roles named in the hospital's MfaRequiredRoles setting (by default the roles the data names) and of anyone holding an administrative permission (admin.* or all-access). Shown here as the defaults.
